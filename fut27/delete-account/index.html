Delete your account

FUT 27 Pack Opener by Devero

You can delete your FUT 27 Pack Opener account and everything saved with it at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Tap the info button at the top of the screen to open About.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and the app returns to its welcome screen.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email hello@devero.se with the subject "Delete my account":

  • for a club saved with Google, send it from the Google address you signed in with;
  • for a guest club, include your guest id (shown under About → Account, with a copy button), or tell us roughly when you played so we can work out together which account is yours.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything we keep for your account: your account id and, for a Google club, the Google account id, email address, name and profile picture link; your sign-in sessions; your coins, cards, packs, squad, drafts and Title Race progress; your purchase records, including the copy at RevenueCat, which checks purchases for us; and the record of your actions in the game. The copy of your game on your phone is wiped too.

Deletion is permanent. We can't restore a deleted club, and coins bought for real money are not refunded when you delete your account. Your Google account itself is not affected; if you sign in with it again later, you start a new club.

What is kept

  • Technical logs on our servers, which can include your IP address, are deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Purchases. Google Play or the App Store keeps its own record of what you bought, under its own rules.

More about how we handle your data is in the privacy policy.
