FAQ

How does Draft work?

Pay a 5,000 coin entry fee and choose a formation. For each of the 18 slots, 11 starters and 7 subs, you pick 1 of 5 random cards. Draft cards are on loan for that draft only and never join your collection.

Then take your squad through a 16-team knockout tournament against real clubs. Opponents get stronger every round, and at half-time you choose a tactic. The further you go, the more you earn, up to 20,000 coins for winning it all. You can save a draft and finish building it later from Club → Saved drafts.

How does Title Race work?

Title Race is a weekly competition for your own Club squad, played against computer-run real clubs. Entry is free, and a week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday. You need all 11 starters in your squad.

First win 4 qualifiers; losses don't count against you. That opens the week's Finals: 15 matches, with opponents that get stronger as you win and easier as you lose. Your rewards depend on how many Finals matches you win, from 2,000 coins up to 50,000 coins plus an Icon Pack and two Premium Gold Packs for winning all 15. If the week ends before you finish, you're paid for the wins you have.

Packs you earn wait unopened under My packs in the Store, so you open them when you like.

How do I recover my account?

A guest club is saved on our server, but only your phone knows which guest it is. If you uninstall the app or switch phones, you lose access to it unless it was saved with Google.

To keep your club safe, open About and tap Save your club with Google under Account. On a new phone, open About and tap Sign in to an existing club, then choose the same Google account.
